Tillora — beta privacy information

Tillora is an experimental receipt and spending tracker. Purchase entries and settings are stored in this app's local browser storage on your device. The app does not require an account or bank connection.

When you choose a receipt photo, the app uses a third-party text-recognition library (Tesseract.js) running locally in the app. Recognition components are downloaded over the internet on first use. The app does not intentionally upload receipt images to our servers; it has no application server. This statement is not an independent audit of the third-party delivery network.

Exported JSON backups are unencrypted. Anyone with the backup file may read your spending records. Uninstalling the app or clearing app storage may permanently remove your records unless you exported a backup.

Beta release only. A public privacy-policy URL, verified disclosures, and developer contact details are required before Google Play submission.
